Overview

Want to experience the 80's?

Come and play Space Pirates, a top-down 2D Game. As you take your ship through the depths of deep space to protect your home from PIRATES. In Space Pirates, you face relentless waves of alien enemies. Eliminate the enemy as you ride and shoot lasers to ensure no hostile force slips past your defenses. As the waves progress, it becomes more difficult every second but with your thinking and strategic maneuvers, you push through enemies to survive. Good luck!!

How to Play

  1. Movement: Move the spaceship with WASD to navigate through deep space and avoid enemies.
  2. Weapons: Press Space to shoot and fire lasers at the enemy.
  3. Health: Your health is indicated by a green healthbar. If an enemy slips past your defenses, you will lose one life. Keep a close eye on your remaining lives. If your lives or healthbar reaches zero, it's game over. There are no items to restore your health, so every hit counts.
  4. Survive: You must survive as enemies increase each wave. Good luck Soldier!!
